Case study Overview
01

Local search — the tourist's side

You share places you already love in your own city. The system translates those choices into vibe, style and values — and produces a match that feels precise, natural and authentic for the experience in Berlin.

02

Building the route — the local's side

The local composes an experience that feels right and personal — by feeling, connection and a real familiarity with the city. The route is built from local intuition and tailored to each tourist.

03

Receiving the route & wandering the city

Every point on the route comes with personal recommendations, small tips and notes from the field. Along the way the tourist can react, ask questions and share feedback in real time — turning the route into a living, dynamic experience.

04

A spontaneous event comes up

During the day, the local can update on events in real time. If they discover something special happening in the city — a show, a one-off unplanned event — they can send the tourist an instant alert with the recommendation.

05

The tourist decides in real time

The tourist receives the notification in real time and can choose whether to add it to the route and visit the place.

The Problem

How do we travel today? Travelers say they're looking for a personal, authentic local experience and end up frustrated by the feeling of missing out (Booking.com, 2023–24).
We mapped three reasons why:

01

Information overload. The traveler is surrounded by recommendations, ratings and lists, but struggles to understand what truly fits them. Choosing becomes a burden.

02

Generic experience. Algorithms offer general solutions that don't distinguish between people, tastes or personal context. The experience loses depth.

03

Lack of connection. There's no link between our life at home and the way we experience a new city. Localness stays an idea, not an experience.

Who it's for

Travelers — curious and open, they enjoy spontaneity and unexpected experiences, and prefer discovering a city through moments, people and atmosphere.

Locals — of every age: students, young people, pensioners.
who want to share their personal knowledge of their city and earn an income on the side.

The Solution

A connection between tourists and locals through personal matching and experiences that feel real.

Personal matching — recommendations built from your own taste and the places you already love.
Local experience — finding the places that let you feel local from day one.
One continuous flow — every stage of the experience lives in one place: simple, clear and intuitive.
